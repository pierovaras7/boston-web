## Excellence in Robotics

Fifth and sixth grade students from Boston Pre-University achieved **first place** in the 2024 National Robotics Olympiad. This achievement reflects the dedicated work of our students and teachers.

### Winning Team

The "Boston Innovators" team consisted of 6 students who worked for three months developing an autonomous robot capable of solving complex challenges.

### Skills Developed

- Logical thinking and programming
- Teamwork
- Problem-solving
- Creativity and innovation

Congratulations to all our students for this incredible representation of Boston.
