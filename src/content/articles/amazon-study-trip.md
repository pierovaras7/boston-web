## Learning in Nature

From September 5 to 12, our secondary school students took a **study trip** to the Amazonian region of Peru. This experience allowed them to apply knowledge in biology, geography, and global citizenship.

### Activities Completed

- Observation of Amazonian fauna and flora
- Encounter with local indigenous communities
- Analysis of environmental impact
- Documentation of the experience

### Global Reflection

Students reflected on issues of sustainability, cultural diversity, and environmental responsibility. A transformative experience that enriches their worldview.

"This trip changed my perspective on the importance of protecting our ecosystems," commented María García, a 9th-grade student.
